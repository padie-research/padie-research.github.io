Institute for Emergent Cosmology & Innovative Technologies (IECIT)

IECIT is a research initiative currently being established in the Philippines, dedicated to fundamental cosmology and enabling technologies for future space-based observations.

Research Focus

  • Quantum gravity and early-Universe cosmology
  • Emergent spacetime and non-fundamental time
  • Primordial gravitational waves
  • Falsifiable predictions in the LISA era

Flagship Framework

QBET — Quantum Bounce with Emergent Time explores a non-singular quantum transition in the early Universe, predicting a distinctive stochastic gravitational-wave background in the millihertz frequency band.
